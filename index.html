Frequently asked questions
What if a restaurant is full?▾
No table is reserved. If a restaurant is busy, simply continue to the next stop and return later. You have until 10:00 PM to complete all stops.
Can I do the stops in any order?▾
We recommend starting at stop 1 at your booked time. After that, you can visit the remaining stops in any order you prefer.
What about food allergies?▾
Please inform each restaurant about any allergies or intolerances when you arrive. Vegetarian and gluten-free options are marked in each stop's details.
What drinks are included?▾
At each stop you get one drink: choose from local wine, sangría, beer, soft drink, water, or coffee.
What if I have a problem with a restaurant?▾
Contact us on WhatsApp immediately and we'll help sort it out. We're available throughout your tour.
